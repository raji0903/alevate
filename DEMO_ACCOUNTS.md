## 🚀 How to Sign In

1. Open the ALEVATE platform in your browser.
2. Click **"Sign In"** in the top navigation bar (or click **"Request Mentorship"** on any mentor card).
3. Enter the desired demo email and password (`password123`).
4. Click **"Sign In"** to access the corresponding portal.
